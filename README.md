# Jak 1: Life Drain
In Jak 1: Life Drain, Jak is slowly drained of all life! Can you beat the game while fighting off Jak's green eco dependency?

## Changes
- You respawn with the maximum amount of green eco pills.
- Your eco pills quickly drain down to zero.
- Once you have no more eco pills, your health will slowly get lower and lower.
- The mod has three difficulties to play with:
  - **Easy**, meant for casual players,
  - **Hard**, meant for a difficult but enjoyable experience,
  - **Calamitous**, meant for an incredibly challenging experience only for the most advanced players.

## On ***Hard*** difficulty,
- Your eco pills drain every 0.3 seconds.
  - For every power cell you collect, this time is increased by 0.002 seconds\
    (up to a total of 0.502 seconds at 101 power cells).
- After your eco pills reach zero, your health will be decreased by one, and your eco pills will be reset to the maximum.
- Eco pills are automatically picked up when they spawn.

## On ***Easy*** difficulty,
- Your eco pills will drain every 0.4 seconds.
  - For every power cell you collect, this time is increased by 0.003 seconds\
    (up to a total of 0.603 seconds at 101 power cells).
- After your eco pills reach zero, your health will be decreased by one, and your eco pills will be reset to the maximum.
- The maximum amount of eco pills is increased to 99.
- Eco pills and green eco are automatically picked up when they spawn.
- Destroying crates and defeating enemies drops more green eco pills
  - Crates drop 2–3 more eco pills compared to vanilla.
  - Enemies drop 2 more eco pills compared to vanilla.

## On ***Calamitous*** difficulty,
- Your eco pills will drain every 0.6 seconds.
  - For every power cell you collect, this time is increased by 0.002 seconds\
    (up to a total of 0.502 seconds at 101 power cells).
- After your eco pills reach zero, your health will drain every 4 seconds.
  - For every power cell you collect, this time is increased by 0.02 seconds\
    (up to a total of 6.02 seconds at 101 power cells).
